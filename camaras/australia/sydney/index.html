Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Save My Play camera system for padel clubs

Are civil works, cabling, or floor trenches required? ▼

No, absolutely zero works. The camera mounts directly onto the court net post or light column in just 5 minutes.

What is the pricing model and what plans exist? ▼

A$295 per camera owned outright. Monthly subscriptions: A$74/mo per court for recording, highlights, and live streaming; or A$220/mo for AI statistics and tactical analysis.

How does the AI process the best shots? ▼

The cameras use AI to process the video stream in real time, automatically extracting top rallies ready to share on mobile.

Is there any long-term contract or lock-in? ▼

Zero contract lock-in. Cancel or pause month-to-month without penalty.

How long does shipping and delivery of Save My Play cameras take in Sydney? ▼

Delivery time to Sydney is approximately 4 weeks from order confirmation. The shipment includes heavy-duty post mounts and quick-connect cables ready for 5-minute setup.

Does the club own the camera hardware outright? ▼

Yes, the hardware is 100% owned by the club via a one-time purchase (A$295). There are no forced hardware leases, and the software plans (A$74/mo for recording or A$220/mo for AI stats) operate on month-to-month flexible terms with zero lock-in.
