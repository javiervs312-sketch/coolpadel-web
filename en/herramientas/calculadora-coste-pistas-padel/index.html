FAQ

How much does it cost to build a complete padel court in 2026?

A turnkey panoramic glass padel court typically costs between €28,000 and €38,000 per court if a concrete base slab is already present. When ground civil engineering, concrete sub-base, or indoor structural canopies are needed, total cost ranges between €45,000 and €65,000 per court.

How fast do new padel courts pay for themselves?

With an average occupancy of 4 to 5 hours daily at €24/hour, a well-run court generates €2,800 to €3,600 in monthly gross revenue. After deducting allocated overheads, initial capital is typically recouped within 14 to 24 months.
