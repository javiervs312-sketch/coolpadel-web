Frequently Asked Questions

All your questions answered about the Save My Play court camera system

Do we need Playtomic API integration?

No integration is required. Save my Play operates autonomously on the court with physical net buttons and QR downloads, ensuring 100% uptime regardless of software changes.

Can players book courts normally?

Yes, court booking runs exactly as usual on Playtomic. Video recording is available directly on-court for whoever is playing.

What internet connection is needed?

A standard Wi-Fi or 4G/5G connection with 5-10 Mbps upload per court is sufficient for HD recording and live streaming.
