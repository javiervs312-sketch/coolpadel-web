Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before ordering custom keychains for your club

What is the ordering process? ▼

1. Send us your vector or high-resolution logo. 2. We prepare a free 3D digital mockup. 3. We produce your real physical sample for €15 (~15 days). 4. Upon your approval, we launch bulk production (from 100 units) with delivery in ~1 month.

What is the minimum order quantity? ▼

Our minimum order is only 100 units per design. Wholesale volume discounts apply at 250, 500, and 1,500 units.

Can we sell them in our club pro shop? ▼

Yes! Most clubs sell them in their pro shop for €5.00 to €8.00 each, generating a healthy margin or gifting them to members in annual packs.
