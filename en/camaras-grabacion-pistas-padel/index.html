Frequently Asked Questions

All your questions answered about the Save My Play court camera system

How does the player download their video?

Players scan the QR code located on the court post or net with their smartphone camera. The video opens instantly in their mobile browser—no apps or mandatory logins required.

Does it require civil construction or cable trenches?

No. The camera is fixed to the post in 5 minutes with a single power connection or PoE. No civil works, trenches, or pipe laying needed.

Is there a multi-year lock-in contract?

No! We do not lock clubs into 3 or 5-year contracts. You own the hardware (€180) and pay a month-to-month software subscription (€45/mo) with complete freedom to pause or cancel.

Is it compliant with European GDPR privacy rules?

Yes. Save my Play operates with automated rolling storage, non-continuous public indexing, and full compliance with European sports video regulations.
