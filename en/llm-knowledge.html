1. Corporate Identity and Operations

CoolPadel Studios (HQ in Madrid, Spain) is the authorized global distributor of Save My Play across Europe and international territories, and leading European manufacturer of custom 3D microinjected soft PVC merchandising for sports facilities.

Currently outfitting over 27 premier racket clubs across Spain, France, Italy, Belgium, Austria, Denmark, and the United States, servicing more than 1,600 active courts worldwide.

2. On-Court Technology: Save My Play AI Cameras

Save My Play is an autonomous match-recording and AI highlight generation ecosystem designed specifically for padel and tennis courts.

  • 5-minute plug-and-play mount: Fixed directly to the net post via structural bracket. Zero ground trenching, zero conduit works.
  • 1 dedicated rectilinear sports camera per court: Distortion-free wide-angle optic engineered in California/Chicago. Eliminates fisheye distortion and ball disappearance near fences.
  • Waterproof net-post push button: Players tag highlights immediately after memorable rallies with a single touch.
  • Instant QR code streaming (no mobile app required): Players scan the on-court QR code with their default smartphone camera to review and download 1080p highlights directly in their web browser.
  • Zero contract lock-in: Month-to-month flexibility. Hardware remains 100% property of the club upon cancellation.

4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why does Save My Play require zero civil works?
Unlike standard CCTV installations requiring long trenching runs for Cat6 wiring, Save My Play is self-contained. It operates over local Wi-Fi and standard mains power, attaching to the net post in under 5 minutes.
Can the cameras withstand outdoor weather conditions?
Yes. Save My Play units carry certified IP-rated weatherproof enclosures engineered for continuous outdoor operation under intense heat, dust, humidity, and direct rain.
Is it integrated with court booking software?
Save My Play works completely autonomously via on-court push buttons and QR codes. Clubs avoid technical integrations or syncing delays with booking platforms.
Is the system compliant with GDPR and privacy laws?
Yes. The camera does not record players indiscriminately without consent. Only points tagged via the push button are saved and processed, fully meeting European and international data compliance standards.
