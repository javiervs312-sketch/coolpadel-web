The 3 mistakes club managers end up regretting

Padel is booming across the UK, with new clubs opening every month.

Most courts look virtually identical, except for the turf colour.

Cameras instantly set you apart from neighbouring clubs.

With a premium feature players love, re-watch and share all over social media.

And always with your club's logo on screen.

Just make sure you don't make these mistakes:

Mistake 1: AI Analytics and Complex Stats

Sounds impressive on paper, doesn't it?

Having an AI break down your technique, count your shots and claim to fix your backhand.

The reality is that by their third match, amateur players realize their heatmap looks just like the previous one.

And counting how many topspin volleys they hit provides zero real value to their weekly Sunday game.

It breaks my heart seeing small clubs in places like Leeds, Bristol or Glasgow paying crazy monthly fees for this.

Paying those rates makes zero financial sense. It's nonsense.

It's just common sense.

Mistake 2: Free trials and 3-year lock-in contracts

Watch out for free trial offers.

Seriously, watch out.

Here is how it usually goes down:

1. During month one, members try it because it's fresh and new.

2. Assuming the hype will last forever, you sign a 2 or 3-year contract.

3. When the initial hype cools down, you're stuck paying every month.

Never mortgage your club's future with a multi-year lock-in contract.

Mistake 3: Most vendors just install generic CCTV security cameras

The match video looks like bank robbery CCTV footage.

Image quality and frame rate are terrible for fast racquet sports.

A quick giveaway of cheap CCTV lenses is court lines.

If court lines look curved like a fishbowl, run away.
