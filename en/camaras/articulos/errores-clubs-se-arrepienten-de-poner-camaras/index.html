The 3 mistakes of clubs that regret installing cameras

Yes, you read that right.

And they are neither one nor two.

I spend the day talking to clubs and these 3 mistakes are repeated over and over again.

I know that investing in cameras is something you don't want to make a mistake with. I recommend that you read it and understand our point of view. So whatever you decide, you will make a more informed decision.

Mistake 1: Analytics and statistics with AI

Does the idea sound cool?

Let an AI analyze your game, tell you how many shots you make and even what you need to improve.

The reality is that by the third match using it you realize that your heat map is quite similar to that of the previous matches.

And that your number of forehands cut with double inverted corkscrews is a piece of information that does not give you much.

It makes me sad to see the money that small clubs in Burgos, Córdoba or Tarragona, for example, pay for this.

It is not profitable to pay those prices. It's crazy.

It's a drawer.

Mistake 2: Trial Periods and Permanences

Be careful with trial periods.

Careful.

This is the usual process:

1º During the first month you see that people use it.

2º Thinking that it will be like this all the time, you sign a long stay.

3º When the initial boom declines, you continue paying.

Don't mortgage yourself with a permanence.

Mistake 3: Most companies use surveillance cameras

The video looks like footage of a bank robbery.

The image quality is terrible.

A good indicator to know if they are surveillance are the lines on the track.

If they are curved, bad sign.

When you do the demo, remind me and I'll show you the difference.
