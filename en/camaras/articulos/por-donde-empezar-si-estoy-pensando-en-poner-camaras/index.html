I'm thinking about installing cameras in my club, where do I start?

I completely understand if you feel a bit lost.

You have probably seen other clubs installing cameras on their courts and you are wondering whether you should do the same.

First of all, I recommend that you be cautious.

Just because everyone else is doing it doesn't automatically mean it's the right move for your club.

Here is what I would do in your shoes:

1º Talk to clubs that already have cameras installed. Ideally clubs on the other side of the country so they have no conflict of interest and tell you the unfiltered truth. Most camera providers list their partner clubs on their websites.

2º Talk to different camera companies and hear what they offer. In Spain and Europe, besides Save My Play (who I work with), there are Rackety, Padmi, AIball, Courtmaster and a few smaller startups. Below you have my calendar in case you want to book a quick call.

3º Gather the feedback from both clubs and providers, compare, discuss it internally and decide. The first decision is not which brand of cameras to buy. First you have to decide whether you actually want cameras or not.

As your first read, I strongly recommend The 3 mistakes of clubs that regret installing cameras.
