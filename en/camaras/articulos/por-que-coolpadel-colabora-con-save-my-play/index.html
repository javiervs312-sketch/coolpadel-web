Why does CoolPadel collaborate with Save My Play?

My favorite part of working in the padel industry is visiting trade shows and talking to founders at every booth.

From knowing the market inside out, I consider Save My Play to be the court camera company with the greatest global potential—and just as importantly, they are honest, reliable people.

Because Save My Play faces huge demand in the US, we teamed up so that at CoolPadel we manage and support clubs across Europe: Spain, France, the UK, Italy, Germany and beyond.
