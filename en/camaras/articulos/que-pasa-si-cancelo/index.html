What happens if I cancel my subscription? Should I keep the cameras?

This is a fairly common question that clubs ask me.

I'll give you a little context in case it's necessary. As this article says xxx. We manufacture the padel cameras. And that is why there is an initial investment of €180 for each camera. One camera per track. We have no permanence. There is no contract, it is a subscription that you can cancel at any time.

And what happens if I cancel the subscription?

Yes, the cameras are yours to keep. The problem is that they only work with our software. So you can't use them for anything else. That's how it is.

Buying a camera and canceling the subscription is a failure for you and also for us. You lose €180. It costs us more than €180 to produce the cameras. Therefore, we are not interested in selling many cameras at once. We also have enough demand to not have to force sales. We recommend trying it on one track and seeing how it goes before trying more tracks. In the long run we both win.

This is how we get 97% of clubs to voluntarily stay with us every month.
