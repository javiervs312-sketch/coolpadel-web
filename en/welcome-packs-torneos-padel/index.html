Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before ordering custom keychains for your club

How far in advance should we order for our tournament?

We recommend contacting us 5 to 6 weeks before your tournament start date to allow sample proofing (~15 days) and serial delivery (~3-4 weeks).

Can we combine two club logos or sponsor emblems?

Yes, we can produce dual-faced keychains or combine tournament and sponsor logos seamlessly on the same racket mold.

What is the cost per player?

At 250 units (e.g. 125 pairs), unit cost drops to €2.50. At 500 units, it drops to €2.00, easily covered by entry fees.
